Hello OneAquaHealth team,

Our Track 3 project, Second Look, now asks the creek check questions in your Citizen Science App's
own words and in its own languages, read from the app's public translation file, so a volunteer in
any pilot city sees the questions they already know. Thank you for making them public.

While wiring them in, we read every translation beside the English for meaning. Most read very
well. A few seem to ask something different from the English, and we thought you would want to
know. We show English for these in our app until you have had a look:

1. Italian and French, "Draining Pipes": the English asks about pipes draining polluted water; the
   Italian ("acqua piovana") and French ("eaux pluviales") ask about rainwater. A clean storm drain
   would be a yes there and a no in English.
2. French, "Construction": "dans ou autour du cours d'eau" (in or around the stream), where the
   English says in the stream.
3. Italian, "Bank Type": "I banchi del canale" may read as shoals or bars, as in "banchi di
   sabbia", rather than the river banks ("sponde" or "argini").
4. "Riffles" in Habitats: Dutch "Geultjes" (small gullies), Norwegian "Strømvirvler" (eddies) and
   Italian "Ruscellamenti" (surface runoff) each seem to name something other than riffles.
5. Norwegian, "Vegetation Type": "Ugress" means weeds rather than herbs or herbaceous plants.
6. Portuguese, "Vegetation Type" left and right: "(primeiros 5 metros)" leaves out "from the
   channel banktop", so people may measure from the water's edge.
7. "Sewage discharge": the Portuguese and Dutch ask about sewage only, while the English also asks
   about any kind of water entry. The Dutch also joins the two with "and" rather than "or".
8. Norwegian, "Anger": "Raseri" reads as rage or fury, stronger than anger ("sinne").

Three small English typos, in case they are useful: "Layed stones with no concrete" (laid),
"recent cuts if vegetation" (of), and the label "Logitude" (Longitude).

Greek is in the file with the start pages but not the questions yet, so we do not offer it.

We have the full list with back-translations and are glad to send it; our repository, with the
list at docs/notes/app_translations.md, opens to the public on Sep 30. We are not translators,
so please take these as questions, not corrections. Happy to change anything on our side.

Thank you,
Alex (Second Look)
