# Thirty minutes at Strawberry Creek

For Alex, on the UC Berkeley campus, with a phone and nobody else needed. The shots feed beats 2, 9 and 10 of `docs/video/VOICE_SCRIPT.md`. UPDATE_14 section 7 item 4 asked for `RECORD_AT_THE_CREEK.md`; this is that file under this name.

## Before you go (2 minutes, at home)

- Phone charged, storage free, lens wiped. Record in 4K or 1080p at 30 frames a second, landscape for B-roll, portrait for the app screen.
- Airplane mode off (the check needs rainfall from Open-Meteo), Do Not Disturb on.
- Open https://second-look-79t.pages.dev/check once so the app is cached.
- Go in daylight, ideally mid-morning on a weekday when fewer people are on the paths.

## Where to stand

Stay on paths and bridges. Do not step into the channel or onto planted banks; the restoration is fragile and it is their creek.

- Faculty Glade (South Fork, by the Faculty Club): open banks and rock, good light, easy for the calm opening shots.
- The Grinnell Natural Area and the Eucalyptus Grove (west side of campus, near the Valley Life Sciences Building): the forks meet near here, and there are built walls and channel sections that show a built bank.
- The west edge near Oxford Street, where the creek goes into a culvert: the strongest built-channel shot on campus.

If a spot is closed or busy, move on. Any concrete wall, pipe outlet or straightened stretch you can film from a path will do.

## The real creek check (shot 6)

1. Start the phone's screen recording, then open https://second-look-79t.pages.dev/check.
2. If the app asks for the two-minute test first, take it for real. Your score travels with the visit.
3. Place the pin yourself at the spot you are standing at, so the location is exact and you chose it.
4. Answer every question from what you actually see. Do not stage anything. If the dry pipe follow-up appears, answer it truthfully.
5. At the end, open the record and tap View as FHIR once, so the badge is on the recording.
6. Stop the recording. Write down the record id from the address bar (`/spot?id=...`). The next session swaps this visit into the README's "See it work" table in place of the golden one.

## What to avoid

- Faces. If a person walks into frame, stop and wait or reshoot. Anyone who must appear signs `docs/release_form.md` first.
- Licence plates on the service roads and at the Oxford Street edge. Frame them out or reshoot.
- House numbers, building numbers and door signs. Campus building names on signs are fine to avoid too; keep the frame on water and banks.
- Student ID cards, laptops with screens, or phone notifications on your own screen recording.
- No AI filters, no beauty mode, no generated fill. Real footage only (hard rule 6).
- Do not say a place is polluted or unsafe. We never state a risk for a specific site.
