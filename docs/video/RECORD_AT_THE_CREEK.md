# Record at the creek

Thirty minutes at Strawberry Creek in Berkeley. Three shots go into the video: the rows at 0:30, 0:40 and 2:10 of `docs/video/SHOTLIST.md`. Two more are spares. Film more than the video uses, so the edit has a choice.

## Release form

Anyone who appears on camera, even only their hands, signs the release form first: `docs/release_form.md`. Print it and bring a pen. Follow every box they tick. If they tick "hands, the phone and the creek, and not my face", keep their face out of every frame. Keep the signed paper safe and out of the repo, because it has their name and signature on it.

## Before you go

- Pick a day with no rain, so the pipe shot matches the dry weather question.
- Phone: Do Not Disturb on, lens wiped, battery well charged.
- Film sideways (landscape), 1080p, 30 frames a second, to match the screen clips.
- Where: the open stretch of the creek on the UC Berkeley campus. In our region pack that is the reach "South Fork, central campus" or "North Fork, campus".
- What the person uses on the phone. From Sep 28, use `/demo?script=1`: the same photos every take, and nothing is stored. Before Sep 28 judge mode is shut, so use the live test at `/t`. The person must be 18 or older, and their answers count as one anonymous session.

## Rules at the creek

- No face of anyone who has not signed the release form. If people walk past, wait until they have gone, or point the camera away.
- No readable car plate or house number in the frame.
- Stay on the path and the bank. Do not step into the water.
- Say nothing on camera about what is in the water or what comes out of a pipe. We never state a risk for a specific site.

## After
