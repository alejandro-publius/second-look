# Shot list: the demo voiced by ElevenLabs, 4:16

What is on screen under each beat of `docs/video/VOICE_SCRIPT.md`. The "Words" column is the same words, and `scripts/tests/test_video_words.py` fails if they differ. The first video, read by Alex over open creek footage, is kept in `docs/video/v1/` with its own shot list.

## How it is built

- A product demo: the app used from the first screen to the last, as a volunteer and then a city analyst would use it, on a phone. Screen recordings fill 198 of its 257 seconds (77 percent); slides are short bridges: slides 1 to 3 for the problem, slides 10, 15 and 16 for four seconds each, and slide 17 to close.
- The recordings come from `apps/web/scripts/record-journey.mjs`: Chrome's screencast at twice the phone's size, so a zoom stays sharp, with every tap and every zoom logged. The cut draws an orange ring where each tap lands and zooms on what the words name, inside a drawn phone frame.
- On the live site wherever the flow allows: the test sitting carries the QA key, so it is stored as a test and never counted; the video walk makes a demo record, never counted and never sent to OneAquaHealth's sandbox; judge mode, `/verify` and `/judges` store nothing. Beside these the cut says "Recorded on the live site".
- A creek check on the live site would be a real record, counted on the city page and sent to OneAquaHealth's sandbox, so the screens that need one come from a local build with the mocked API: the follow-ups after nine dry days, the record with each answer beside its score, the city page's pipes, referral and lab result example, and the return check with the creek's visits. Beside these the cut says "Sample records, made up for this video."
- The slides are AI illustrations Alex made with ChatGPT image generation, trimmed of their white borders; slides 13 to 17 are wide strips, panned panel by panel. Slides 9, 12 and 13 were patched to match the words and the app, from the originals in `~/second-look-media/slides/orig/`.
- Captions are burned in, in the written forms, a sentence at a time, and `second-look.srt` is written beside the video. One file, 1920 by 1080, 30 fps, 4:16: the cover, the 17 beats and a credits card.
