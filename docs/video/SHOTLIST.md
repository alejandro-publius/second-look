## Notes

- **Numbers.** Every number Alex says is read off the README or the named `results/` file on the recording day. Beat 7's slot is filled from `results/model_pass_table.json` after the real run; if no model passed, say "None of them passed a feature, so none of them may speak." Beat 10's zero errors comes from `results/fhir_validation.json`.
- **Scratch voice.** The voice in the rough cut (`docs/video/rough_cut_scratch_voice.mp4`, built by `make video-rough`, never committed) is macOS `say`, not Alex. It is there only to check the timing. Alex records the real voice against `docs/video/teleprompter.html`.
- **Title cards and missing clips.** The rough cut puts a 3 second title card before each beat and burns each line in as a caption, above the footage credit; the cards are not part of the 3:45. A footage clip or screen recording that is missing becomes a grey card that names it.
- **Credits on screen.** Every footage clip carries its own credit line in the lower left corner: title, author, licence, Wikimedia Commons. Keep it in the final cut.
- **Licence.** Several clips are CC BY-SA, so the finished video is released under CC BY-SA 4.0. The end card says so, and so do `docs/devpost.md` and `docs/video/CREDITS.md`.
- **Our footage, their footage.** None of the creek footage is ours. The words say "creeks like this one" or name the creek shown, and never call it our visit or our record. The Strawberry Creek photos are Strawberry Creek, by Coro. The outfall is called an outfall and nothing else.
- **Extra clips.** `make video-clips` also records judge mode (with the clock set after the lock, in the recording only), the score filter on `/spot` and `/judges` on a desktop, for any cutaway Alex wants. Beat 9 uses one of them, `extra-check`, the check on a phone.
- **The sandbox.** Anyone can delete records there. Record beat 11 soon after a re-push. If their sandbox does not answer on the day, cut beat 11 and say nothing about it.
- **Words.** Say "audit log", never "blockchain". Never say what comes out of a real pipe: we do not state a risk for a specific site.
- **Faces.** No clip shows a face: `scripts/cut_footage.py` has Apple Vision look at two frames a second, and `docs/video/footage_fetched.json` keeps what it found. The Bagmati River clip has a man seen from behind, with no face; the part used, 1.5 to 3.5 seconds, keeps him out of frame, or at most at its edge.
